## What it shows

Three iPhone frames on light grey, showing one fitness app's flow. Screen 1 ("Home"): a greeting header
("Hello James! 👋" plus a blue "Pro Member" pill, avatar top-left, bell and kebab icons top-right), then a
"Today Starts / See All" section with a wide white card holding a circular progress ring (grey track,
black/blue arc) beside a small legend list (Exercise 220/600min, Stand 140/600min). Below it, two
side-by-side cards: "Calories 234/848kcal" with a green horizontal bar-segment tracker and two stats
underneath (grams carbs / grams fat), and "Activity Tracking 234/848kcal" with a small blue outlined
squiggle line-chart and two stats (Done / Left). Below that, a horizontal image card ("Today's Challenge",
a progress bar, "+32%" badge, "Beginner" pill) over a workout photo. Bottom: a pill nav bar with a
labelled active "Home" segment (icon + text in a black rounded pill) plus three unlabelled icon-only tabs.

Screen 2 ("Activity Plan"): a horizontal date strip (dimmed dates either side of a highlighted blue
"Today, 25 Nov" pill) above a list of rounded white/pale rows, each a left icon-in-tinted-circle, two-line
label (title + subtitle), and a right-aligned bold number + unit -- repeated for Weight Step, Goal Step,
Heart Step, Muscle Step. Below, two more full-width cards (Weightlifting task-count + progress bar;
Steps with a big number).

Screen 3 ("Challenge complete"): back-arrow and "+" circular buttons top corners, "Great Work! / Your
Daily Challenge is Almost Done! 🔥" heading, a "Wellness Score" row with a big circular gauge (blue/green
arc, chevron-navigation either side, checkmark and number badges around the ring, a bold "6.5/10 Good" in
the centre), then a "Today Starts / See All" row of two video-thumbnail cards with kcal/min stat overlays.

## Why it's here

The paired "Calories" + "Activity" stat cards side by side (bar tracker vs line chart, each with two
sub-stats) is a direct, buildable template for an Intake home screen showing eaten-vs-burned at a glance.
The icon-in-circle list row with a trailing bold number+unit is a good pattern for a macro or nutrient
breakdown list, and the circular multi-metric gauge (checkmarks + numbers ringed around a central score)
is worth adapting for a "daily nutrition score" summary.
