## What it shows

Left: an iPhone frame on light grey, an AI-assistant home screen. Header "Hi Daniel 👋 / How can I assist
you today?", a rounded search-style input ("Ask anything…", magnifying-glass left icon, a filled black
circular send/arrow button on the right). A "Quick Actions" label over a 2x2 grid of white square tiles,
each a small pastel icon-in-tinted-circle plus a centred label (Summarize, Translate, Generate Ideas,
Rewrite). Below, "Recent Actions / See all" -- a list of white rounded cards, each a bold question title, a
grey one-line answer preview, a small clock icon, and a "1h ago" timestamp.

Right: two separate floating panel snippets on the same grey backdrop (not phone-framed) -- a "Total
Queries" card with a "Last 7 days" dropdown and a light-blue-filled line chart (soft area fill under the
line, gridlines, one highlighted data point as a hollow blue-ringed dot) over date labels; and a chat
input bar (plus button, "Type a message…" placeholder, mic icon, filled dark circular waveform/voice
button). A separate "Key Takeaways" card lists three flat grey rounded rows of bullet-style text with a
copy icon top-right.

## Why it's here

The 2x2 "Quick Actions" tile grid (icon + label, pastel tint circles) is a fast pattern for common
logging shortcuts (e.g. "Log meal", "Scan barcode", "Log water", "Repeat last"). The clean area-line chart
with one highlighted data point is a solid reference for a 7-day calorie-trend graph, and the
"Key Takeaways" flat-row card is worth adapting for a plain-language daily nutrition summary.
