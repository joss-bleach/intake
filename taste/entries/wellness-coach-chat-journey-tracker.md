## What it shows

Three iPhone frames on a sage-green gradient background, dark rounded status-bar pill visible at the top
of each. Screen 1 ("Chat"): a horizontal date strip (numbered day pills, one solid dark "today" highlight)
under a "Your Coach / Always here to help" header row with a small leaf-like icon. Below, a chat transcript
-- pale-green rounded bubbles (left-aligned, coach) alternating with white rounded bubbles (right-aligned,
user, small avatar), plain conversational copy ("Ready to start my journey!", "Welcome! I'm your wellness
coach. How are you feeling today?"). A bottom composer bar: plus icon, "Type a Message" placeholder,
waveform/mic icon, and a solid dark-green circular send button.

Screen 2 ("Journey/Motivation"): heading plus subtext, then two side-by-side stat cards -- "Water" (icon,
big "1,3L" figure, grey target caption, a segmented horizontal bar made of many thin vertical ticks,
mostly filled dark) and "Weight Loss" (big "60kg" figure, a small two-column Current/Now readout, a percent
progress line). Below, a "Weight Trend" card: a bar chart (Mon-Sun, most bars pale grey, one bar solid
dark-green and taller, annotated with a floating "70kg"/"75kg" callout pill above the tallest bars) with a
one-line grey encouragement caption above it.

Screen 3 ("Settings"): heading + subtext, then two white cards -- "Profile" (icon, email field, Age/Height
two-column inputs) and "Goals" (icon, Current Weight/Goal Weight two-column inputs, a "Goal Type" dropdown
row reading "Maintain Weight", and a partially visible "Diet Preference" field below the fold).

All three share a bottom pill nav bar with three labelled segments (Chat / Journey / Settings), the active
one rendered as a solid dark rounded pill with icon+text; inactive ones are icon+text on transparent.

## Why it's here

This is the closest reference in the batch to Intake's actual domain: a conversational coach flow paired
with a settings screen that has literal "Goal Weight" and "Diet Preference" fields. The segmented-tick
water-intake bar and the bar chart with one highlighted day plus a floating weight-callout are both
directly reusable for calorie/weight trend visualisation, and the three-tab Chat/Journey/Settings nav is a
plausible top-level IA for a chat-driven logging experience.
