## What it shows

Marketing-style presentation of a single "Smart Ring / AI Skin Protection App" dashboard screen, phone
mockup centred over a moody, photographic teal-and-slate background (a wet rock with small white flowers
growing from it, soft volumetric light streaks). Headline text ("Smart Ring", "AI Skin Protection App")
top-left, small caption labels ("3 Core Metrics Tracked", "24/7 Skin Monitoring") in the corners of the
backdrop — these are marketing annotations, not app UI.

Within the phone frame: header "Dashboard" with a search icon top-right, on a translucent light overlay
over the photo background (frosted-glass card, not solid colour). Below it, a stat block: small-caps
label "COLLAGEN INDEX", a large bold "72%" figure, a "+18% vs yesterday" caption, and a small inline
sparkline chart to the right tracing recent values with a single highlighted dot. Beneath, the actual
photographic hero image continues (the ring product sitting on the rock among flowers) framed within the
card as if the metric panel floats over a real-world photo. Below that, a horizontal reminder banner
(frosted glass pill) reading "SPF 50+ Next reminder: 12 min" with a "Remind me" button on the right.
Under the banner, three equal glass-morphism tiles in a row, each with a small icon, bold label ("Critical
/ UV Index 8.6", "Hydration / 62%", "Barrier / Normal") and secondary caption. Bottom nav is a minimal
four-icon row (home, log/book, leaf, profile) with no visible labels or container, floating directly on
the photo.

## Why it's here

The frosted-glass metric panel floating over a full-bleed photographic background (rather than a flat
colour dashboard) is a distinctive art direction worth considering for a hero "today" screen — pairs a
single hero number with a sparkline and a horizontal actionable reminder banner ("Next reminder…" +
button), a pattern directly reusable for meal or water reminders in Intake.
