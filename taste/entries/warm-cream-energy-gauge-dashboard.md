## What it shows

Full-screen dashboard on a plain white background. Top row: a circular profile photo, top-left, and a
pill-shaped light-grey icon button holding a calendar glyph, top-right. Below that, a large black
condensed-sans headline spanning two lines, "Let's track your progress", with a hand-peace-sign emoji
inline at the end of the second line — a greeting, not a metric.

The centrepiece is a pale-cream rounded card labelled "Energy Total" (small, top-left of the card) holding
a wide semicircular gauge (roughly 240° of arc, opening at the bottom): a ring of short thick tick-mark
dashes rather than a continuous stroke, coloured amber/gold for the filled portion and solid black for the
unfilled remainder, so progress reads as a colour split in the dash pattern rather than a filled arc length.
Centred under the arc: a small circular amber badge with a white flame icon, then a large bold number
"986", then a smaller grey caption "Kcal Gained" below it.

Below the gauge card, the meal list begins directly (no gap card) as a single continuous white rounded
panel: a "Breakfast" section header with a circular "..." overflow button on the right, then stacked meal
rows, each a small rounded rectangle tinted a very pale colour (cream for one, blush-pink for the next)
containing: a square thumbnail photo on the left, then in the row's body a bold food name ("Greek Yogurt",
"Granola") with a small pencil/edit icon at the far right of that line, then on the line below the calorie
count in bold ("350 KCAL", "200 KCAL" — number bold, unit small-caps grey) followed by three small pill
icons with gram values (a spiky orange dot, a feather/leaf, and an amber droplet, presumably
carbs/protein/fat). A "Lunch" section is cut off at the bottom of the screen, obscured by a floating
pill-shaped bottom nav bar: a filled black home-icon circle on amber background at the far left, then three
outline icons (a stacked-rectangles/toggle icon, a bar-chart icon in a bordered square, a person icon) — a
floating tab bar overlapping the content rather than a fixed docked bar.

## Why it's here

The dash-pattern semicircular gauge (ticks changing colour rather than a filled stroke) is a distinct take
on the calorie-ring idiom worth having alongside more conventional ring treatments. The tinted-per-row meal
list (each food card its own soft pastel background rather than uniform white rows) and the greeting
headline pairing with the metric card are both usable patterns for Intake's home/log screen.
