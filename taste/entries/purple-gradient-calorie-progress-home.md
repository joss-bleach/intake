## What it shows

Home dashboard on a very pale lavender background. Header row: circular profile photo, left; centred solid
indigo/purple pill button with a small gold crown icon and white text "Go To Premium"; white circular
notification-bell button with a red unread dot, right.

Below that, a full-width white pill search bar (magnifying glass icon, grey placeholder "Search") paired
with a small white square button holding a "sliders"/filter-settings icon.

Main card: a large rounded rectangle filled with a purple-to-indigo diagonal gradient. Top-left: a white
circular badge with a compass/target icon and label "Your Progress". Below, a huge bold white "92%",
smaller white date text "28 February 2025" beneath it. Right side of the card: a circular ring gauge
(two-tone — pale lavender track, solid white arc for progress) with centred white text "1480" (large bold)
over "Calories" (small).

Next, a white rounded row card: a small rounded-square icon badge (crown) on pale lavender, bold text
"Completed 4 Goals" with grey subtext "Keep Upgrading to Get Benefits" beneath.

Below that, a three-column white rounded card, one column per macro (Carbs / Protein / Fat), each with a
label + "..." overflow button, a bold "used/total g" fraction (e.g. "281 / 359g"), and a thin horizontal
progress bar underneath in that macro's colour (green, orange, purple respectively) against a pale-grey
track.

"Planned Meal's" section header with a right-aligned indigo "View All" link, then a list of white rounded
rows — square food photo thumbnail, bold label ("Add Breakfast"), grey subtext ("Recommended | 540-715
Kcal"), and a circular outline "+" button on the right of each row.

Bottom: floating indigo circular FAB (stacked-lines icon) overlapping a docked white rounded-top tab bar —
Home (active, indigo-filled icon+label), Analytics, My Diet, Account.

## Why it's here

The gradient hero card combining a headline percentage, a date, and a calorie ring all in one surface is a
strong "today at a glance" composition. The three-column macro row with inline thin progress bars
underneath a bold fraction is a compact, scannable macro-summary pattern worth carrying into Intake's home
screen, and "Planned Meal's" as tappable add-rows (not yet-logged placeholders) is a useful proactive-log
pattern.
