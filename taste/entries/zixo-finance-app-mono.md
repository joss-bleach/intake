## What it shows

Three-screen finance app ("ZIXO" by Juice Lab), rendered in an almost fully monochrome, foggy
white-to-grey palette with soft diffuse lighting — no bright accent colour anywhere, only near-black text
and thin grey chart bars for contrast.

Screen 1 (onboarding): status bar mock at top, "ZIXO" wordmark, "Skip" link top-right. A stack of
overlapping card previews (peeking behind each other, fanned slightly) showing balance/transactions
content, floating above the fold. Below, large serif-leaning headline "All your Finances, simplified in
one place." Then two full-width pill buttons stacked: solid near-black "Continue with Google" with a
Google "G" icon, and outlined white "Continue With Apple" with an Apple icon; a small "Already have an
account? Log in" link underneath.

Screen 2 (home/dashboard): round avatar top-left, "ZIXO" wordmark centred, bell icon top-right. A card

## Why it's here

The near-monochrome palette with only text weight and one darker/coloured highlighted bar to draw the eye
is a striking restrained alternative to colour-coded dashboards — worth considering as a calmer visual
direction for Intake if the goal is to de-emphasise numbers rather than gamify them. The
highlighted-single-bar-with-callout-tag technique in the bar charts is a clean way to call out "today" or
"this meal" within a longer trend without extra chart chrome.
