## What it shows

A dashboard mockup on a rich purple-to-lavender gradient background (deep violet at top fading to pale
lilac by mid-screen). Header: "Welcome Back" with a waving-hand emoji, a large bold headline "Stay On
Track Today", a notification bell and round profile photo top-right. Below, a horizontal week strip of
seven pill-shaped day cards (day name over date number); the past two days carry a small purple
checkmark badge in the corner, the current day ("Sat 14") is fully filled solid purple, others are
white with a dashed outline for future days — a clear done/today/future visual language.

A large white card follows: "Today's Goal" label, big bold "460 / 1600" calorie count, and a stylised
gauge icon on the right — a thick black C-shaped arc wrapping a smaller circular flame badge, functioning
as a stat icon rather than a literal progress ring. Below a divider, three equal sub-cards for
Protein/Carbs/Fat show grams-left as a bold number, a label, and a small circular ring gauge each in a
distinct accent colour (orange, purple, green) partially filled. A "Today's Meals" section follows with
white rows per meal (name, flame-icon kcal range, stacked overlapping circular food thumbnails with a "+"
at the end). A floating bottom tab bar with five icon buttons (home, dining, scan/add, chart, bowl) sits
pinned at the very bottom.

## Why it's here

The done/today/future week-strip treatment (checkmark badge vs. solid-fill vs. dashed-outline) is a
compact way to show adherence history inline with date navigation — more informative than a plain
calendar strip. The stacked-overlapping-thumbnails-plus-"+" pattern for a meal's logged items is a space-
efficient alternative to a full list, worth considering for Intake's meal-slot summary rows.
