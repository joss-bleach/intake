## What it shows

Two-screen dark fitness app, near-black background, purple-to-magenta gradient accents.

Screen 1 (home/dashboard): top bar "Hello, Alex / Let's build something great" greeting left, round
avatar with a small notification-count badge and a hamburger-menu button right. Below, a large dark
rounded card with two small pill tabs ("Today" selected, "May 17" as a date pill) top corners. Centred, a
big circular progress ring — thick gradient arc (magenta to purple) over a dark track — with "You are at
56% Daily Goal" stacked centred inside it, and a tiny running-figure icon marking current position on the
ring. Below the ring, a one-line encouragement in a rounded pill: a star icon + "Great job! Only 28% left
to reach your daily goal." A small pagination dot pair beneath indicates more cards via swipe. Under the
hero card, two side-by-side stat tiles ("Step 4,360/8,000" with a purple footprint icon in a circular
badge, "Active calories 320/600" with a flame icon), each a compact dark card with an icon-badge,
label and bold value/target line. Bottom: a floating dark pill tab bar with 5 icon-only buttons (home
selected/filled purple square, a book/log icon, calendar, bell, profile).

Screen 2 (Community/leaderboard), shown as a tilted/angled phone mockup: header "Community" with an info
circle icon, and a calendar icon button top-right. Below, a ranked vertical list of team/group cards, each
row: rank badge ("#1"–"#6"), a square photo thumbnail, group name (e.g. "Morning Movers", "Strong
Together", "Peak Performers"), member count caption, and a points value + "pts" label right-aligned in a
darker pill. The current user's own group ("#3 Peak Performers … You") is highlighted with a full-width
magenta-to-purple gradient fill and white text, standing out from the otherwise plain dark rows above and
below it. Same floating icon tab bar at bottom, profile icon now selected/filled.

## Why it's here

The circular daily-goal ring with an inline encouragement pill directly below it (star icon + short
supportive copy tied to the live percentage) is a strong, low-effort way to make a single daily number
feel motivating rather than clinical — very applicable to a calorie-goal ring in Intake. The
highlighted-own-row treatment in the leaderboard (gradient fill breaking an otherwise uniform list) is a
reusable way to make "your position" pop out of any ranked or grouped list.
