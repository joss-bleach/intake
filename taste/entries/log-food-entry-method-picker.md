## What it shows

A bottom-anchored modal sheet layered over a partially-visible dashboard (warm off-white background,
system Live Activity pill overlapping the status bar, a "Weekly Momentum" gauge and a "Log your meal"
row card peeking through above). The sheet has a warm cream background, "Log food" as a bold serif-ish
heading top-left and a plain X close icon top-right — no drag handle. Below sits a 2x2 grid of equal-size
white rounded-rectangle cards, each holding a centred line-icon and a bold label: magnifying-glass-plus
icon over "Search items", camera icon over "Photo or scan", a lined-document icon over "Text
description", and a clock/history icon over "Previous logs". The top two cards each carry a small
pill-shaped badge floating above their top edge, dark green/black background with white text: "Most
accurate" over Search items, "Fastest" over Photo or scan — an explicit trade-off label attached directly
to the option, not explained elsewhere. All four cards are visually equal weight (same size, same
white-on-cream card style); only the floating badges signal a recommended default.

## Why it's here

The 2x2 entry-point grid with a floating "fastest / most accurate" trade-off badge on the two most
relevant options is a clean way to let a user pick a logging method without a written explanation —
directly reusable for Intake's own "how do you want to log this meal" entry screen (search vs. photo vs.
free text vs. reuse-a-past-log).
