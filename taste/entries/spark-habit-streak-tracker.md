## What it shows

Three-screen sequence for a "Spark" habit-tracking app on a warm orange-to-cream vertical gradient
background (deep burnt-orange top fading to pale peach bottom), consistent across all screens.

Screen 1 (onboarding): near-empty gradient card, bold serif-ish sans headline "Welcome To Spark / Build
Streaks & Track Your Habits" left-aligned near the bottom, with two decorative horizontal rule marks
next to "Streaks" and "Track" words. Below it a full-width pill button, white fill fading to orange, a
flame icon in a circle on the left, "Get Started" centred, chevron arrows `>>>` on the right.

Screen 2 (home): top bar has a round profile photo, top-left, "Good Morning J.Snow" greeting, and two
circular icon buttons top-right (bell/notification, hamburger menu). Below, a large bold two-line
headline "Keep the streak alive, spark your daily motivation." Then a vertical stack of three white
rounded-rectangle cards, each for one habit: a small flame-icon badge + "N Days Streaks" label top-left,
bold habit title below ("Read 20 pages habit book"), a fraction-style progress caption ("27/60 days
completed"), and on the right a small dot-matrix heatmap grid (filled orange/red dots vs empty grey dots)
visualising completion history at a glance. Each card has a rounded checkbox top-right for marking today
done. Bottom of screen: a floating pill-shaped tab bar with 4 circular icon buttons (flame/home selected
and filled solid orange, trophy/medal, plus/add, profile), the selected item raised/filled.

Screen 3 (trophies/achievements): header "Trophies" with the same bell/hamburger icons. Below, sections
grouped by habit name ("Read 20 pages...", "Write UX design...") each with a small trophy-count badge
top-right (e.g. "02"). Under each heading, a horizontally scrollable row of square white achievement-badge
cards: a laurel-wreath-and-star icon or a trophy-cup icon centred, an "Unlocked"/"locked" orange pill
tag with a small link icon, and a streak-length caption below ("07 days Streaks"). Locked badges are
rendered in a faded/translucent orange tint versus solid orange for unlocked ones. Same floating pill tab
bar at the bottom, now with the trophy icon selected/filled.

## Why it's here

The dot-matrix heatmap on each habit card is a compact way to show a completion history inline without a
separate chart — worth adapting for a weekly/monthly adherence view in Intake. The gamified,
unlock-badge-per-milestone treatment (locked vs unlocked visual state, streak-length captions) is a strong
reference for turning logging streaks into a rewarding, low-pressure ritual rather than a guilt trip.
