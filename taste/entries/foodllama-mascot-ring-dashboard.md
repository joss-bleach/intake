## What it shows

Dashboard on a plain white/very-pale-grey background, branded "Foodllama" in bold black top-left, with a
white circular person-icon button top-right. Centred below: a huge bold black calorie number "2,129" and
grey subtext "82% from 2,570 calories goal" — the primary metric stated as a sentence rather than a
labelled stat.

The centrepiece is a large circular ring gauge, but instead of a plain progress arc, a soft grey rounded
"halo" arc sweeps most of the way around (roughly 300°) ending in a small water-droplet-shaped white
terminus icon on the right — read as the goal track. Inside the ring sits a 3D-rendered cartoon llama
mascot head wearing colourful rainbow sunglasses and a brown pom-pom/beanie-style wool tuft on top,
smiling — the mascot occupies the ring's centre in place of a numeric readout. Around the ring's edge, three
small circular icon badges sit at different points along the arc like satellites: a blue stopwatch/clock, a
yellow house/building icon, and (implied) a third — each presumably tapping into a different stat. To the
left of the ring, outside it, a small stat block: a black running-shoe icon, bold "6d", grey "streak"
caption underneath.

Below the ring, a macro-split summary as inline coloured-bold text on one line: "21% protein" (blue), "38%
carbs" (green), "41% fat" (yellow) — no bars or rings, just coloured percentage text separated by plain
grey connector words.

Below that, a smooth filled area/stream chart (not bars) plotting the same three macros as stacked colour
bands (blue/green/yellow) over seven days, with a dashed horizontal goal line at "2,570" labelled on the
left in a yellow pill, and a small circular avatar marker (llama-with-sunglasses, matching the mascot)
pinned at the current day's peak on the line. Day labels below the chart: date number bold, weekday
abbreviation small below it (e.g. "6 / WED"), current day highlighted in a light grey rounded pill.

Bottom: a floating black rounded-full toolbar, icon-only, five items — pencil (manual log), bookmark, image
frame, camera, and "..." overflow — overlapping the top of a food-entry list that's just starting to show
below (a small food photo thumbnail visible at the very bottom edge).

## Why it's here

The character mascot living inside the progress ring instead of a number is a strong personality/mascot
direction — worth flagging for Intake if the product wants warmth over pure data. The smooth stacked-area
macro trend chart (vs. bar chart) is a softer alternative to the bar-chart pattern seen elsewhere in this
batch, and the floating icon-only bottom toolbar (pencil/bookmark/photo/camera/more) is a clean multi-entry-
method logging affordance.
