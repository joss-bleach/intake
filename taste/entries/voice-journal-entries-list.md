## What it shows

A single iPhone frame (visible physical buttons and Dynamic Island) on a soft pink-to-blue blurred gradient
background. The app itself, "Voice Journal", is plain white/off-white with large left-aligned black
serif-adjacent title text. Below the title, a full-width rounded search-bar-style input reading placeholder
"Record a new entry" with a solid black circular microphone button docked at its right edge.

Below that, a vertical list of entry cards, each: a bold black headline in quotes-free sentence form
("Can't quiet my head tonight"), a grey metadata line (weekday, date, duration -- "Sun, Dec 15 · 2:23"), a
horizontal row pairing a black circular play button with a waveform visualization (vertical bars of
varying height, mid-grey), then one to two lines of grey body/transcript text, and finally a light-grey
rounded tag pill ("Gratitude") on the left with a trash icon on the far right.

## Why it's here

The single-input "record a new entry" bar with an inline mic button is a strong pattern for a voice-based
food-logging entry point (parallel to typing a meal description). The waveform-plus-transcript card layout
and the small category tag pill are directly reusable for showing a logged voice entry with its
auto-transcribed/parsed text underneath.
