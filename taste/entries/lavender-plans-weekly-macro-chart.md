## What it shows

A "Plans" screen on a soft lavender/periwinkle background. Header row: white rounded-square back-arrow
button, left; centred bold black title "Plans"; white rounded-square button with a vertical three-dot
overflow icon, right.

First card, white, rounded: "Losing Weight Goal" title with an overflow "..." button, then three stat
columns side by side, each a big bold black number with a small unit and a grey caption underneath: "56.0
kg / Goal Weight", "-0.45 kg / Goal Rate", "-0.5 % / Goal Rate".

Section label "Your Progress" in bold black, left-aligned, on the lavender background (not inside a card).

Second card, white, rounded, taller: "Coached Program" title with overflow button, then a legend row of
three small coloured square swatches with labels — green "Crabs" [sic, likely "Carbs"], orange "Protein",
light-purple "Fat" — and a right-aligned "Weekly" dropdown pill with a chevron. Below, a stacked bar chart,
one bar per weekday (Mon–Sun), each bar built from three separately-rounded pill segments (green at the
bottom, orange in the middle, purple on top) rather than one continuous stacked bar, so each nutrient
segment reads as its own rounded pill stacked with gaps.

Below that, a 2×2-ish grid of smaller white rounded stat cards, each with a small circular icon-badge (a
heart on pale pink for "Heart Rate" 97 Bpm, a droplet/radar icon on pale blue for "Water" 1200 ml, a shoe
icon for "Walk Steps", a moon/cloud icon for "Sleep"), each card title paired with an overflow "..." button.

Bottom: a floating circular indigo FAB (icon: horizontal-lines/settings glyph) overlapping a docked white
rounded-top tab bar with four icon+label items — Home, Analytics, My Diet, Account — house, up-trending
line-chart, ticket/document, and person icons respectively.

## Why it's here

The three-separately-rounded-pill-segments stacked bar (rather than one continuous stacked bar) is a nice
detail for a weekly macro-trend chart — worth reusing for Intake's analytics view. The plain
number-over-label stat-column pattern (goal weight / goal rate / goal rate %) is a clean way to present a
weight-loss goal without extra chrome.
