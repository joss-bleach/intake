## What it shows

"Coach" tab home screen, part of the same app as the orange Journal screen (matching bottom nav labels
Coach/Journal/Profile and identical macro-goal values of 92g/208g/264g/27g). Top area: large serif-leaning
bold headline in dark navy, two lines, "Today is going to be a good day." with a white pill badge top-right
holding a small flame icon and "0". Behind/below the headline, a flat vector winter landscape illustration
spans the width of the screen — pale pink-to-blue sky gradient, a soft orange sun/glow low on the horizon,
white rolling snow hills, two simple dark-green triangular pine trees on the left, a small brown A-frame
cabin with a lit yellow window and a wisp of white smoke from its chimney just right of centre, and one
more pine tree on the right — a calm, seasonal, editorial-illustration mood rather than a data-forward
hero.

Below the illustration, on plain white background: "My goal" bold black section header, then a white
rounded card with four small ring-gauge badges in a row (same style as the Journal screen: yellow/Fat,
pink-red/Protein, sky-blue/Carbs, brown/Fiber), each showing "0 / goal g" since nothing's logged yet.

Next, a white rounded row: a small blue bathroom-scale icon in a rounded square, bold "My daily weigh-in"
label, and a circular outline "+" button on the right — a single-purpose quick-add row.

"My tools" section header, then two large square tiles side by side: a blush-pink tile with a salad-bowl
illustration icon, bold "Recipes" and grey "More than 300 recipes" subtext; a mint-green tile with a
clipboard/checklist icon, bold "Articles" and grey "More than 500 articles" subtext — a content-library
entry point rather than a logging action.

Bottom nav: Coach (active), Journal, Profile.

## Why it's here

The full-width seasonal illustration paired with a mood-setting headline ("Today is going to be a good
day.") is a distinctive way to open a coaching/home tab with warmth before any data appears — worth
considering for Intake's zero-state or daily-open moment. The two-tile "Recipes / Articles" content-library
entry point is a reusable pattern for surfacing non-logging content without cluttering the primary
tracking flow.
