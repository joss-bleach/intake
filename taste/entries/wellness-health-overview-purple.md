## What it shows

Three-screen light-mode wellness/health app, off-white background, deep indigo/navy-purple as the primary
accent, with soft pastel secondary cards (light blue, cream/tan).

Screen 1 (home): "Good Morning, Alex / Here's your health snapshot" greeting left, round avatar right, no
button icons. Large indigo rounded card "Wellness Score" with a bold "82 / Very Good" figure, a circular
ring gauge on the right (white ring on indigo, small heart icon centred) and a one-line encouragement
below ("Keep it up! You're on the right track."). Below, a "Heart Health" row card with a heart icon,
chevron to expand, a bold "72 bpm / Resting Heart Rate" figure and a small multi-bar sparkline in indigo.
Then two square stat tiles ("Sleep 7h 24m / Good" with bar sparkline in purple, "Steps 6,842 / Today"
with bar sparkline in green). Bottom nav: five items (Home selected, Insights, a raised solid indigo
circular + FAB in the centre breaking the bar line, Care, Profile), icon-over-label style on white.

Screen 2 (mood check-in): hamburger icon and bell (with red dot) top corners. Large friendly heading
"How are you feeling today?" with a soft pink circular blob graphic (heart outline) top-right, and small
supporting copy "Track your health. Understand your body. Live better." Below, a horizontally scrollable
filter-pill row (All selected/filled dark, Heart, Mind, Sleep, Activity as outlined pills). Then a large
indigo "Heart Rate" card with bold "68 bpm / Today, 8:30 AM" and a full-width line chart (white line over
indigo, axis-labelled 40–120 on the right, times along the bottom). Below, two secondary rows: a light
blue "Stress Level / Low / Calm and balanced" card with a lotus icon, and a cream "Energy Level / Good /
Keep maintaining" card with a lightning icon. Same bottom nav with centre FAB.

Screen 3 (Health Overview): title "Health Overview" with a kebab-menu icon. Segmented control (Today
selected/filled dark, Week, Month). A 2x2 grid of pastel stat cards: light-blue "Blood Pressure 118/76
mmHg Normal", cream "Heart Rate 68 bpm Normal", pale-blue "Blood Oxygen 98% SpO2 Normal", cream
"Temperature 36.6°C Normal" — each with a small relevant icon top-right and a "Normal" caption in muted
grey beneath the value. Below, a white "Heart Rate Variability" card with a value ("45 ms"), a "Balanced"
tag, and a full-width line chart with 0/30/60 y-axis gridlines. Then a light-blue "Hydration" card: "1.6 L
/ Goal: 2.5 L" text on the left, a small circular progress ring ("64%") on the right.

## Why it's here

The filter-pill row (All/Heart/Mind/Sleep/Activity) for switching what a hero chart shows is a reusable
control for an Intake "today" screen that might toggle between calories/macros/water. The 2x2 pastel
metric grid with consistent icon-value-status structure, and the small ring-plus-numbers hydration card,
are both directly adaptable for a nutrition-overview screen with several simultaneous targets.
