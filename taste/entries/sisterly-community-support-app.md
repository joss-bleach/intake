## What it shows

Three iPhone frames (visible hardware buttons) over blurred warm/cool photographic backgrounds
(sunset flowers, mossy green light, dusty rose), for a women's support community app "Sisterly". Screen 1:
a top progress/step indicator bar, a small logo mark, italic serif wordmark "Sisterly" centred, then three
stacked feature rows below it (icon, all-caps bold label, one-line grey description) -- "Closed Groups",
"No Judgment", "Support" -- all set over the blurred photo, with a full-width dark-purple rounded button
("Find your group") anchoring the bottom.

Screen 2 ("Your space / Choose what resonates now"): a vertical stack of glassy translucent cards over the
green-toned background, each labelled with a small emoji + "Group" eyebrow, a large serif headline (e.g.
"Motherhood", "Moving / New country", "Divorce / Separation"), a one-line description, a row of overlapping
circular avatar thumbnails with a participant count, and a full-width translucent white "Join" button.
Bottom tab bar: three pill segments (Space / Dialogues / Profile) in mismatched accent colours.

Screen 3 (a single story/post): a "Back" link and "Published" date top row, a large curly quotation mark,
a big serif pull-quote as the headline, an attribution line (avatar, name, age), body paragraphs in
regular grey text, three pill action buttons ("♡ Support", "Reply", "Save"), then a full-width dark
"Write your story" button and a small "You can write anonymously" caption.

## Why it's here

The translucent glass-card-over-photo treatment and serif pull-quote styling give a warm, human,
non-clinical tone -- useful counterpoint to typical data-dense nutrition UI. The topic-card list with
avatar-stack + participant count + single CTA is a reusable pattern for community/challenge features if
Intake ever adds social or group elements.
