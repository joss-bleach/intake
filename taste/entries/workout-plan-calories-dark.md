## What it shows

Three-screen dark-mode fitness app on a near-black background with a vivid purple/violet accent.

Screen 1 (home): top bar with round avatar and "Good Morning! Guy Hawkins" greeting left, search and bell
icons right. Below, a large purple rounded rectangle hero card — "My Plan For Today" headline, a circular
progress ring on the right showing "25%" in the centre with a thin yellow/gold arc for progress against
a purple track, and a small "2/8 Complete" caption. Then a "Start new goal" section: a horizontally
scrolling row of image cards (workout photos with a rounded white play button overlaid), each captioned
with exercise name, muscle groups, duration and calorie burn as small pill-like text/icon pairs
(clock icon + "35 min", flame icon + "120 cal"). Below that, a "Today task" list of rows, each with a
thumbnail, exercise name, duration/calorie captions, and a circular play button on the right. Bottom tab
bar: Home (purple, selected), Discover, Activity, Settings, each icon over a small label, on a dark
rounded bar.

Screen 2 (My Plan): back chevron top-left, centred title "My Plan", "Statics" text button top-right.
"Daily Plan" heading with a 2x2 grid of colour-coded stat cards (purple "Calories 720 Kcal" with flame
icon, dark "Steps 2,000" with footprints icon, green "Sleep 6 Hours" with moon icon, blue "Water 10 Cups"
with droplet icon). Below, "Goal in progress" list of exercise rows identical in style to screen 1's task
list.

Screen 3 (Calories Details): back chevron, "Calories Details" title. Large circular ring gauge centred,
gradient from white to orange, showing "753 KCAL OVER" in bold centre text, flanked left/right by plain
numeric stats "3541 EATEN" and "854 BURNED". Below, a "See stats" dropdown chip, then a row of three
macro trackers (Carbs/Fat/Protein) each as a label, a thin horizontal progress bar in a distinct colour
(pink, orange, purple), and a "current/target g" caption. At the bottom, a dark card with a large emoji
face (tired/neutral), "Day rating" heading, a one-line coaching tip ("Eat more healthy carbs to boost
your day rating!") and a "See more" pill button.

## Why it's here

The "Calories Details" screen is close to a direct template for Intake's daily nutrition summary: a
single over/under ring gauge flanked by eaten/burned numbers, three colour-coded macro bars beneath, and
a friendly emoji + one-line coaching tip card at the bottom turning a plain number into gentle feedback.
The colour-coded 2x2 stat grid on the plan screen is a reusable pattern for a daily-glance dashboard.
