## What it shows

Three-screen light-mode health-tracking app (blood sugar monitoring), pale grey-lavender background,
white cards, small rounded icon badges in pastel colours (lilac, pink, blue).

Screen 1 (home): top bar with round avatar, "Good Morning [wave emoji] / Wyatt Anderson" greeting, bell
and hamburger icons right. Below, a large lilac card labelled "Blood Sugar" with a green "Normal" status
pill top-right, a huge bold "208 mg/dL" reading, and a small red/black bar-chart sparkline to its right.
"Overview" section header with left/right nav arrows (one filled black circle). Two square stat tiles
side by side ("Steps 4,399" lilac card with a small line-sparkline background, "Sleep 7h 30min" pink card
with a moon icon). Below, a "Blood Sugar Trend" card with a "Weekly" dropdown and axis labels ("120",
"108", "Friday"). Bottom: floating pill tab bar, home icon selected in a black circle, plus a bag/log
icon, pie-chart icon, profile icon, and a separate raised blue circular FAB (+) breaking out of the bar on
the right.

Screen 2 (History): "History" title, calendar icon top-right. Below, a search bar ("Search your records…")
with a filter/sort icon button. A "Select Date" row shows a month ("July 2026") with chevron nav, then a
horizontally scrollable strip of date chips (day-of-week + number, selected date "20 Saturday" highlighted
in a filled rounded rect). A "Select Time" row shows a similar horizontal strip of hour marks (7:30–10:00)
as a tick/bar timeline with a highlighted current time marker. "Daily Summary" section: three circular
meal-photo avatars (Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner) each with an mg/dL reading beneath. Below, a list of log
entries — circular photo thumbnail, reading value, a green "Normal" status pill, chevron to view detail.

Screen 3 (Reports): back chevron, "Reports" title, refresh/export icon right. Segmented control (Daily /
Weekly selected / Monthly) as a pill switcher. Large semicircular gauge arc coloured in three zones
(blue/normal, yellow/high, red/very high) with the current reading "250 mg/dL Overview" centred beneath
the arc and a small dot marker on the arc showing position. Legend below with three coloured dot + label +
percentage rows (Normal 70-130 56%, High 131-180 28%, Very High >180 16%). "Average Blood Sugar" bar chart
beneath: dotted/pattern-fill bars for most days, one bar solid black to mark the selected/current day, day
labels underneath, with a small callout tag above the highlighted bar showing its status.

## Why it's here

The three-zone colour-coded semicircular gauge with a percentage-breakdown legend is a clean way to
summarise a distribution (e.g. days in/out of calorie or macro target) at a glance. The date-chip +
hour-tick horizontal timeline pair on the History screen, and the dotted-vs-solid bar chart that highlights
just the selected day, are both strong candidates for Intake's log history and weekly trend views.
