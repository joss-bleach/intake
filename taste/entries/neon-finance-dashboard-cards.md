## What it shows

Three iPhone frames over an oversized "Finance" wordmark and a bright blue-sky/grass illustrated
background. Screen 1: a lime-green card headed "Financial Dashboard", a big "$10,7k / Total Balance"
figure beside two circular icon buttons (link, bar-chart), two pill action buttons ("Withdraw" ↗ /
"Deposit" ↙) side by side, a month selector (Aug/Sep/Oct text row with a dot indicator under the
selected month), and a "Transactions" stat block (bold "37 / This month") with a row of overlapping
circular avatar thumbnails plus a "+7" overflow bubble.

Screen 2: a black card with a yellow "Monthly Limit" panel containing a semicircular speedometer gauge
(black arc on yellow, thin needle) and a big "$6,469/10k 64%" readout, then two side-by-side pill stat
tiles ("Withdraw $1,780.32" green tag, "Deposit $4,688.32" green tag) and a green upsell card ("Get Plus",
placeholder body copy, price, circular arrow button).

Screen 3: a "Deposit" screen -- circular avatar with a small badge, name + masked card number + edit
pencil icon, two side-by-side pill buttons ("QR Code" green, "Share link" yellow), and a "Transactions /
See all" list of rows (avatar, name, date, right-aligned dollar amount).

All screens share the same top bar pattern: circular back-chevron (or nothing) top-left, a circular
"grid/apps" icon top-right.

## Why it's here

The bold color-blocked cards (acid green / black / yellow) and the semicircular speedometer gauge for a
budget/limit are a strong reference for a punchy, non-clinical calorie or macro "budget remaining" visual.
The stacked stat-tile pairs with small coloured trend tags are reusable for showing eaten vs. burned
calories at a glance.
