## What it shows

Three iPhone frames on a warm peach-to-sage gradient, for a skincare app "Glowup". Screen 1: a large
circular-cropped photo of a woman applying a face patch, with three small pill "tag" labels overlaid at
angles around its edge ("Skin Analysis", "Product Scanner", "Personal Routine", each with a tiny icon
chip), a top-left "Glowup" wordmark, bold black headline "Build Your Perfect Skincare Routine" (one word,
"Skincare", underlined with a hand-drawn squiggle), grey subtext, and bottom-row "Skip" text plus a solid
black circular next-arrow button.

Screen 2 ("Today's Routine"): avatar and gift-icon top row, bold headline "Today's Routine to
Protect(Skin)" with the last word circled, a horizontal day-of-week strip (numbered date pills, one
highlighted solid dark), a "First steps" checklist of three white rounded rows (icon-in-circle, label,
trailing circular checkbox -- one shown checked black), then a "Daily Plan" section beginning with a dark
pill-shaped icon-tab bar (five circular icon buttons, one highlighted green).

Screen 3 ("Insights"): a search bar ("Search cosmetics") beside a small scan-icon button, a "Skin care
Trends / See more" section with one large gradient photo card (person mid-skincare-routine, "Nutrition"
and "2 Min" tag chips top-left/right, a bottom caption + circular arrow button), then a "Focused Skin
Massage / See more" list of horizontal thumbnail rows (photo, title, step-count + duration meta).

## Why it's here

The checklist-of-daily-steps card with icon-in-circle rows and a trailing checkbox is a clean pattern for
a "log your meals today" checklist. The day-strip date picker with one solid highlighted pill and the
dark pill-shaped icon-tab bar (five circular buttons, one accent-highlighted) are both reusable navigation
components worth adapting for Intake's daily log view.
