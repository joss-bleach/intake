## What it shows

Two iPhone frames on a soft blue-lavender gradient backdrop. Left: an "Insights" screen (small-caps
"Analytics" eyebrow above a large serif-ish "Insights" title) with two stacked white cards. The first,
"Activity Rings", shows a line chart (two overlaid lines, one purple one orange, on a dotted-grid
background) with a big "72 bpm avg" figure top-right and hour labels (9am-9pm) along the x-axis; below
the chart a three-column stat row (Resting / Peak / Average, each a bold number over a small grey label).
The second card, "Sleep Breakdown", repeats that exact pattern with a 7-day purple/orange/light-purple
bar chart (legend dots for Deep/REM/Light) and the same three-stat row underneath. A pill-shaped bottom
tab bar with four icon buttons (house, small chart, a filled black square-grid "active" icon, person) sits
at the bottom, the active tab rendered as a solid black rounded pill.

Right frame: a "Profile" screen -- name ("Tanzim Ahmed"), member-since date, and a blue "Premium Plan"
badge at top. Below it, a white card with three quick stats (Height / Weight / Age) separated by thin
vertical dividers, then a "Health Goal" card with a label, percentage, and a horizontal gradient progress
bar (purple to orange) with a white circular handle. Below that, two grouped list sections ("Health" and
"App") of white rounded rows, each with a circular icon-in-tinted-circle, a bold title, a grey subtitle,
and a trailing arrow-out icon. Same pill tab bar at the bottom, person tab active this time.

## Why it's here

The three-stat row under a chart (bold number, small grey label, thin dividers) is a clean, reusable
pattern for showing calorie/macro breakdowns (e.g. "Eaten / Burned / Remaining"). The gradient progress
bar with a draggable-looking handle is a strong candidate for a daily calorie-goal indicator, and the
grouped icon-row list is a tidy template for a settings or profile screen.
