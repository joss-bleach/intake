## What it shows

A dashboard mockup on a plain white background with a sage-green accent card. Header: round profile
photo, small grey date line "Today 15 Dec", bold "Good Morning" beneath it, no notification icon visible.
Below, a two-option segmented control fills the width — solid black pill "Today" (selected) beside a
plain light-grey "Weekly" — a simpler binary toggle rather than a scrolling date strip.

The main content is one large sage-green rounded card: a week-day letter/number strip (S M T W T F S,
current day "W 12" picked out in a solid black pill), then a big centred arc gauge — black stroke tracing
most of a semicircle — with three stacked values inside: an apple emoji + "1200 Eaten" on the left, a
flame emoji + "1200 Burned" on the right, and the plain number "1200 Cals" centred and largest. Below the
card, three equal flat-colour macro chips (pale blue "Carbs 160/225g", pale green "Proteins 80/112g",
pale peach "Fats 35/50g"), each just a label and an X/Y gram fraction, no ring or bar. A full-width pale
sage "Generate My Day" button (with a sparkle icon) follows — an AI-assisted meal-planning CTA presented
as a primary action, not tucked into a menu. Below that, a "Today Meals" list: a "Breakfast" section
header with running kcal total and a plain "+" add icon, then item rows with a square food photo
thumbnail, item name, kcal value, and a checkbox + X pair on the right for confirm/remove. A large black
circular floating "+" button overlaps the list, and a bottom tab bar (bowl, chef hat, flame, person
icons) sits pinned at the base.

## Why it's here

"Generate My Day" as a full-width primary button (not buried) is a strong pattern for surfacing
AI-assisted planning as a first-class action rather than a hidden feature — directly relevant if Intake
offers AI meal suggestions. The combined eaten/burned/net arc gauge with three labelled values in one
shape is a denser alternative to separate rings, and the checkbox+X pairing per logged item (confirm vs.
remove) is a simple micro-interaction worth reusing.
