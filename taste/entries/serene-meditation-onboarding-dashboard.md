## What it shows

Three iPhone frames on a muted lilac-grey background, for a meditation app called "Serene". Screen 1: a
full-bleed onboarding illustration -- a stylised person in profile, eyes closed, hands in prayer, rendered
in a soft airbrushed purple-to-peach gradient with a hazy cloud silhouette at the base -- over large
centred black serif headline "Welcome to Serene Your Calm Space!" and a grey subtext line, anchored by a
full-width dark-purple rounded pill button ("Let's Get Started" with a chevron).

Screen 2 ("Home"): a hamburger-menu icon and greeting ("Welcome, Nick / You're Growing…") with a bell icon,
then a white card pairing a left-aligned progress list (three rows -- Mindfulness 12min/20min, Habits Done
3/5, Sleep Hours 6.5h/8h -- each an icon, underlined coloured label, and grey value) against a right-aligned
large circular ring gauge (purple-to-orange gradient arc) with "100% Remaining" centred inside. Below,
a "Categories / See All" row of four icon tiles (Mindfulness, Habits, Wellness, Fitness, each a small
illustrated icon over a label), then a "Recommended For You" full-width gradient image card ("Calm
Breathing", play icon). Pill nav bar with five icons, active one in a white circle outline.

Screen 3 ("Mindfulness" category page): back-chevron and kebab-menu icons, "Find Your Calm" heading, and a
vertical stack of full-bleed photo cards (women in yoga/breathing poses over warm gradient overlays), each
with a bottom-left two-line title and a play button bottom-right.

## Why it's here

The ring-gauge-paired-with-list-of-metrics card (three underlined progress rows beside one big gradient
ring) is a compact way to show several running totals (calories, protein, water) plus one hero number at
once. The soft airbrushed illustration style and warm gradient photo cards suggest a gentler, less clinical
visual tone worth considering for Intake's onboarding.
