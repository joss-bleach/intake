## What it shows

A mockup-style dashboard (device frame visible, plus a floating "Search this screen" pill at the very
bottom suggesting this is a design-reference capture rather than a live app) on a peachy-cream
background. Top row: round profile photo, bold greeting "Hello Alex" with a waving-hand emoji, subtext
"Get ready", and a notification bell with a red dot, top-right. A white rounded card labelled "Daily
Intake" with an orange-to-green gradient pill badge reading "4 Day" sits below; inside, a horizontal
slider-style progress track running from a green dot through an orange-filled section to a pale unfilled
end, a round orange handle marking current position, and "Progress · 55% · 100%" captioned beneath.

Below that, two equal side-by-side rounded cards in flat colour blocks: a dusty-pink "Step to Wall" card
with "4,500 Steps" and a footprint icon inside a circular ring in the bottom corner, and a sage-green
"Drink Water" card with "12 Glass" and a water-drop icon in a matching ring. Under those, a horizontal
day-of-week strip (Sun-Sat) with the current day circled in orange. Finally a list of meal rows begins —
each a rounded colour-tinted card with a small circular food photo on the left, meal name ("Breakfast",
"Lunch") and a kcal range in the middle, and a white pill "Add" button on the right.

## Why it's here

The slider-track (not ring or bar) treatment for daily progress with a coloured handle and dual-value
caption is a distinct visual idiom worth having on file. The two-tone flat-colour stat cards (dusty-pink /
sage-green) for steps and water, and the kcal-range-per-meal-slot list ("Breakfast · 488-536 kcal") rather
than a fixed target number, are both patterns Intake could borrow for a lighter, softer dashboard mood.
