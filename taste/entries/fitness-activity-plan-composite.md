## What it shows

A single marketing composite showing three related phone screens side by side from a fitness/step-tracking
app (not calorie-first, but adjacent — steps, walking plans, activity goals).

Left screen, "Overall" (share/summary card): white back-arrow and "X" close buttons flank a centred title
in the header, with a thin blue progress/step-indicator bar beneath it (a multi-step flow dot/segment
indicator). Below, a large full-bleed photo card — a blurred-motion running-shoe action shot in warm
orange/red tones — with a date overlay top-left ("Tue, 16 Dec/26") and a huge bold white "80" with "Steps"
caption beneath, bottom-left of the photo. Under the photo, a white stat row with three columns (icon +
value + label): distance in miles, calories, floors. A full-width solid-blue rounded button below reads
"Share" with a small forward/share-arrow icon — this screen is a shareable end-of-activity summary card.

Middle screen, a home/plan dashboard: circular avatar photo top-left, "Good evening." bold greeting with
grey subtext "tomorrow's [walking-icon] walking slot is", white bar-chart icon button top-right. Two stacked
full-width rounded pills, blue then orange, each showing a bold time ("05:55 AM", "06:55 AM") with a small
circular swap/refresh icon overlapping the boundary between them — presented as two alternative slot
options. Below, "Today's Goal" header with a right-aligned "View Plan" link, then a white card: "Steady
Running" label, small icon stats row (distance, 60min duration, 10,500 step target), and a horizontal
slider/progress track underneath. Below that, a full-width orange weather-style card: temperature "66.8F"
and "Cloudy" with icon, top-left; a circular badge with a bell icon, "05:25 AM" and "Sunrise" caption,
top-right; and a translucent progress strip at the bottom showing "82%" and a paired stat.

Right screen, "Plan Breakdown": white back-arrow, centred small blue-bag "Plan Breakdown" label pill with
icon, white "X" close button. A grey-bordered card: "Week 1" caption, bold "Intermediate Daily Activity
Plan" title, and a "Goal:" description line in grey. Below, a scrolling list of white rounded day-cards —
"Day 1: Moderate Walk", "Day 2: Power Walking Session", "Day 3: Running and Walking Mix", "Day 4: Active
Rest Day with Light Walking" — each with a small icon stats row (distance/duration/step-count) and a thin
horizontal progress bar underneath, bar-fill length varying per day to suggest a multi-week plan overview.

## Why it's here

The end-of-session shareable summary card (big number over a full-bleed photo, single clear "Share" CTA) is
a strong pattern Intake could reuse for a shareable daily/weekly nutrition recap. The two-alternative-slot
picker (stacked pills with a swap icon between them) and the day-by-day plan-breakdown list with per-day
progress bars are both transferable to a structured meal-plan or goal-plan view.
