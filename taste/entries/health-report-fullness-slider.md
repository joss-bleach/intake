## What it shows

Three iPhone frames on a soft pink/purple gradient. Screen 1: a near-blank splash/loading screen -- just a
centred gradient (purple-to-pink) heart-shaped logo mark on a pale background, no other content.

Screen 2 ("Home"): a greeting row ("Hello Jhon 👋 / How are you feeling today?" with avatar and bell icon),
a "Today's activity log / See all" section of four equal square tiles in a 2x2 grid, each a
tinted-circle icon (alarm clock, cloud, running figure, thermometer) over a bold time and a grey caption
("8:00 AM / Morning walk", "12:30 PM / Lunch time", etc). Below, a "Today's suggestions for you" section
with three narrower square tiles (icon, bold title, small grey description) -- "Mindful moment", "Community
insight", "Download report". Bottom: a pill nav bar with a filled blue circular active icon plus three
outline icons.

Screen 3 ("Health report"): a heading with bell/profile icons, a three-way segmented toggle (Weekly /
Monthly / Yearly, active segment a filled purple pill), then a bold state label ("Satisfied / Feel
comfortable") above the standout element: a hand-drawn-style blue-outline stomach illustration with a
vertical slider track running beside it, labelled rungs from "Hungry" at the bottom through "Satisfied" to
"Very full" at the top, and a purple circular handle on the track marking the current position. A full-width
purple "Next" pill button anchors the bottom.

## Why it's here

The vertical fullness/hunger slider against a stomach illustration is an extremely direct fit for Intake --
it's essentially a hunger-or-satiety self-report control, exactly the kind of subjective input a
nutrition app might want alongside objective calorie counts. The 2x2 activity-log tile grid and the
segmented Weekly/Monthly/Yearly toggle are both solid, reusable patterns for a history/insights screen.
