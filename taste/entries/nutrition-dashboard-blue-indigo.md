## What it shows

A dashboard mockup on a plain light-grey background. Header: small grey "Good morning." over a bold name
"Abraham Steevan", a blue notification bell and round profile photo top-right. Below, a full-width grey
rounded search bar with a magnifying-glass icon and "Search..." placeholder — search treated as a
persistent dashboard element, not hidden behind a nav icon.

The centrepiece is a large indigo-to-blue gradient card: a header row "Count Your Daily Calories" with an
avocado emoji and a vertical three-dot overflow menu top-right; inside, on a slightly darker blue inset
panel, "Eaten 3,143" in small text above a huge bold "285" with "KCAL LEFT" beside it, then a horizontal
progress bar (solid blue fill labelled "65 KCAL" inline, remainder shown as a white diagonal-hatch/stripe
pattern rather than plain grey — a distinctive "remaining" texture). Below the card, three equal white
rounded stat cards for Carbs/Protein/Fat, each with a label, a three-dot menu, a bold "X / Yg" fraction,
and a thin flat progress bar underneath in a matching accent colour (green, orange, purple).

A "Planned Meals" section follows with a blue "View all" link, then rows per meal: a circular food-emoji
icon in a light tinted circle, meal name ("Add Breakfast", "Add Lunch", "Add Dinner"), a grey "Recommended
| kcal-range" subtitle, and a circular blue outlined "+" button on the right. A pill-shaped bottom tab bar
floats with five icons, the centre one an oversized solid green circular "+" button raised above the
others as the primary action.

## Why it's here

The diagonal-hatch texture for "remaining" budget on the progress bar (instead of flat grey) is a small
but distinctive detail that makes "how much is left" legible at a glance. Meal rows framed as "Add
Breakfast / Add Lunch / Add Dinner" with a recommended kcal range before anything is logged is a good
empty-state pattern for Intake's own planned-but-not-yet-logged meal slots.
