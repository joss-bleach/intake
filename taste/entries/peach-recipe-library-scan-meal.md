## What it shows

A recipe/meal-library screen with a peach-to-white vertical gradient filling the top third and fading to
white by mid-screen. Header row: white circular back-chevron button, left; white pill button, right, with
a small book icon and "Food Log" label.

Two large equal-width white rounded action tiles side by side, each centred content, icon above label: a
line-art steaming pot icon with "Create New Recipe", and a corner-bracket viewfinder frame around a small
apple icon with "Scan a Meal" — a camera/scan affordance styled as a framed-object icon rather than a
literal camera glyph.

Below that, a full-width white pill search bar with a magnifying-glass icon and grey placeholder "Search
recipe...", then a horizontal row of filter chips — "All" (implicitly selected/first), "Breakfast",
"Lunch", "Dinner" — as bordered rounded-full pills, no visible active-state styling distinguishing a
selected chip from the rest.

Recipe list below: each entry a white rounded card, two-column layout — left column has a bold small-caps
category label ("Breakfast", "Lunch", "Dinner"), a bold recipe name below it ("Combo Cheesy Sunny Side-up",
"Beef Tacos", "White Egg Toast"), an underlined coral-orange "Detail Composition" link, then a row of three
small pill badges with icon + gram value for macros; right column is a large square food photo with a
dark-scrim rounded badge in its bottom-right corner showing the calorie count ("475 Kcal", "560 Kcal", "655
Kcal") in white bold text directly on the photo.

Bottom nav is a plain (non-floating) row of four unlabelled icons on white: a small house-with-tree
illustrated icon, a chef's-hat icon (active, with a soft peach glow behind it), a bathroom-scale icon, and
a person icon.

## Why it's here

Calorie count overlaid as a scrim badge directly on the food photo (rather than as separate text) is an
efficient, photo-forward way to surface calories in a list. The "Create New Recipe" / "Scan a Meal" paired
action tiles at the top is a strong pattern for Intake's primary meal-logging entry points, and the
underlined "Detail Composition" link is a lightweight way to route into a macro breakdown without a full
disclosure control.
